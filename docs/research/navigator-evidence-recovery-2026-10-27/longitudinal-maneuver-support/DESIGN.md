# Original controlled low-speed response characterization

Use only verified historical original20-segment maneuver recording and exact contiguous alert phases. No controller modification. Characterize three weak-stop and six20mph brake-step repeats separately. Original command, control, status, torque and state stay on logged publication/send/receive clocks, not ECU internal actuation clocks. Alert start/end are phase markers; actual command onset/withdrawal is measured separately.

Report per-repeat original command range, pre-step speed/acceleration, response at.1/.2/.3/.6/1/2s, peak deceleration, command-to-status/torque onset, and pressure/torque clearance/return of positive propulsion after phase end. Explicit three-received-frame persistence for status-clear and torque≤0/50Nm; these descriptive cutoffs are not controller safety constants. Search bounded10s after phase end; mark missing/censored. Reject driver/invalid intervals rather than turn them into identified response. Fit a simple held-out-repeat temporal acceleration template (first two train, third test) within each identical phase family, compare last-prephase acceleration persistence. No inference of independent gas/brake Jacobian from coissued commands, no inactive-coast counterfactual, no highway extrapolation or authority model installation.
