Valid negative/near-zero gas requests must match within0.025m/s². At least3 distinct episodes across2 other routes. Collapse within-episode slices before empirical1st/99th percentiles plus0.15m/s² descriptive residual floor. These are research choices, not validated vehicle tolerances.

The tentative proposal requires coast upper response <= target+0.10m/s² and lower >=target-0.30m/s², with its worst tracking error at least0.01m/s² better than brake's best error. No proposal on urgent demand, stop/FCW, stale/closing lead or uncleared actual/arbitrated pressure. A proposed lease is <=200ms and0.01m/s demand-area, latches until owner disable and does not release an already committed brake.
