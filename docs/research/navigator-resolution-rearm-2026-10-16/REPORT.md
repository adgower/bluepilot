# Resolution-aware quiet rearming — local prototype

October 16, 2026. Report-local prototype only; not integrated into paired branches or selected on the Comma. Owner settings and 1.20/0.97 remain unchanged.

## Change

The existing rearm gate requires one second of continuously fresh/valid quiet demand and measured curvature. Its adjacent-sample slope tolerance was smaller than common 0.1° wheel-signal increments. `ResolutionAwareRearm` allows **one 0.1° wheel-signal quantum**, converted through the current fixed pinion model at current speed, in that adjacent-sample check:

`allowed adjacent change = existing slope limit × elapsed time + one curvature quantum`

A difference of two quantized samples can contain one quantum of rounding uncertainty. The allowance also admits some real motion within that quantum; it does not prove that physical motion is absent. The unchanged whole-dwell range cap prevents accumulating this allowance unboundedly across samples.

Unchanged: one-second dwell; 40–60 ms cadence; valid/fresh context; driver, lane-change and feedback gates; absolute quiet curvature0.0005/m; whole-dwell range0.0001/m; one-shot refill;0.00025rad·s pre-limiter ramp budget; correction magnitudes; feedforward; native safety; and all final output bounds. No timer reset at ordinary pauses and no unconditional refill or larger budget were added.
