A useful uncertainty-qualified trim needs repeatable natural turning episodes whose full recent history resolves signed excess beyond the existing uncertainty bound, with fresh source provenance and stable independent wheel/body agreement. Both directions, several independent turns, normal transitions and missing installed-source70+coverage remain needed for any broader steering claim.

## Separate future hypothesis: target-angle tracking

The failed body-uncertainty-qualified hypothesis does not prove useful midrange feedback is impossible. A separate bounded angle-error-led policy could compare fresh ECU angle against the atomic requested angle, propagate sensor and target/calibration uncertainty, and use lag-aware prediction/release rather than requiring positive body excess above2×yaw uncertainty. That distinct objective was subsequently implemented locally as B1and B2under `wheel-led/`. B1completed21focused raw segments but failed a hypothetical quantized feedback gate. B2passed227unit checks yet still failed the32case delayed/quantized gate, so no B2raw route or full Bcorpus replay was run. Their negative receipts appear below and in their separate FINAL_STATUS.jsonfiles.

There is observable angle error in the selected steady sample: directional CAN-angle minus atomic target median3.048deg(p10/p90 2.325/3.705deg; minimum0.7155deg), versus median offset-corrected target magnitude19.497deg. The underlying Ford signal is `SteeringPinion_Data.StePinComp_An_Est`, an ECU compensated pinion-angle estimate with nominal0.1degDBC resolution. Quantization alone is much smaller than this residual, but does not bound ECU estimate bias, compensation, filtering, calibration or target error. Its quality flag also needs explicit treatment.

The requested angle depends on learned ratio/stiffness/roll/offset, and their learning consumes the same motion estimate used in body screening. Perfect tracking of a biased target can still give the wrong physical response. Target-vs-vehicle-model agreement proves conversion consistency, not physical correctness. A future angle loop must resolve that target uncertainty, manage actuator delay and interaction with the ECU's hidden loop, and use body evidence as a separately designed response constraint/veto. It should not disguise a global factor cut as feedback. Parameter uncertainty propagation must include its units, frame, temporal correlation and target-model systematic error. `paramsd.py:get_msg` first setsP=sqrt(diag(filter covariance)) and publishes parameter standard deviations from that vector. An initial reading of the later field assignments alone incorrectly described them as variances; reading the localPdefinition corrected that mistake. No parameter-estimator defect is established.
